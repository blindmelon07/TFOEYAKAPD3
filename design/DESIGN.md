## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a dignified, prestigious, and institutional presence rooted in fraternal honor, civic service, and lifelong brotherhood. Built specifically for institutional administration, member directories, and community stewardship initiatives, the interface blends classical heraldic authority with modern administrative clarity.

### Personality & Values

- **Dignified & Institutional:** Command respect through structured proportions, balanced geometry, and authoritative editorial typography.
- **Fraternal Stewardship:** Emphasize solidarity, public service, and organized community initiatives through clear information architecture and deliberate spatial hierarchies.
- **Noble Prestige:** Utilize profound deep navy grounds with calibrated warm metallic accents, evoking ceremonial weight without sacrificing legibility or digital utility.

### Visual Style

The aesthetic synthesizes modern institutional structure with subtle dimensional discipline:

- Crisp architectural lines anchoring complex membership registries and fraternal metrics.
- Controlled low-opacity ambient layers and hairline metallic borders that reference heraldic brass and gold-leaf without ornamental excess.
- High-contrast readability, ensuring that leadership rosters, civic resolutions, and ledger records remain prominent, orderly, and indisputable.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the deep institutional authority of maritime blue and fortified by ceremonial gold, charcoal neutral grounds, and ivory surfaces.

### Primary & Accent Roles

- **Primary Navy (`#0F2347`):** Represents structural authority, organizational governance, and solemn brotherhood. Used for key action buttons, formal banners, dominant typography, and major headers.
- **Secondary Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Represents honor, distinction, and fraternal merit. Used strictly for badges of rank, distinguished member milestones, active focus rings, and accent dividing rules.
- **Tertiary Civic Indigo (`#1E3A8A`):** Serves as an interactive supportive shade for hovered primary elements, secondary tags, and functional graphical data.

### Neutral Foundation & Functional Tones

- **Canvas Base (`#F8F9FA`):** Soft institutional ivory surface providing comfortable reading contrast for lengthy documentation.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces for records, credential profiles, and ledger panels.
- **Structural Neutral (`#1C2024`):** High-density charcoal used for primary text content, avoiding pure black to preserve organic print-like refinement.
- **Border Neutral (`#D8DEE4`):** Crisp, subtle gray for hairline structural borders and institutional dividers.

## Typography

The typographical framework contrasts the ceremonial weight of `Merriweather` with the precise utilitarian clarity of `Inter`.

### Hierarchy Application

- **Serif Headings (`Merriweather`):** Applied exclusively to organizational statements, ceremonial greetings, chapter charters, and card section headers. Captures traditional, literary, and civic heritage.
- **Sans-Serif Utility (`Inter`):** Applied to data grids, membership IDs, navigation menus, and dense narrative bodies. Ensures high legibility during operational reviews, member auditing, and mobile use.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Small labels and metrics use `label-sm` with a strict `0.05em` letter-spacing treatment, reflecting formal insignia engraving.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial discipline adheres to an 8px base rhythm that emphasizes clean symmetry, structured margins, and formal alignments.

### Grid Architecture

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max-width of `1280px` centered on canvas, standard gutter of `1.5rem` (`24px`), and outer boundary margin of `2.5rem` (`40px`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid presentation scaling from `768px` to `1024px`, utilizing `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Unified single-column stacking below `767px` with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` page edge gutters to maximize viewing surface for legal text and records.

### Spacing Philosophy

Negative space is treated with architectural intention: generous between distinct functional modules (`space-xl`), balanced inside content vessels (`space-lg`), and compact within metadata clusters (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subdued, disciplined elevation rather than dramatic floating planes, projecting structural permanence and institutional reliability.

### Depth Strategy

- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat ivory background (`#F8F9FA`) establishing a steady, paper-like ground.
- **Card & Container Tier:** Surface `#FFFFFF` offset with a crisp 1px perimeter outline (`#D8DEE4`) and an ultra-subtle ambient downward cast (`rgba(15, 35, 71, 0.04) 0px 2px 4px`).
- **Raised Interactive Layer:** Applied during hover states on actionable cards and data rows: transitions to an ambient drop of `rgba(15, 35, 71, 0.08) 0px 8px 16px`, coupled with an assertive hairline border shift to Primary Navy or Gold.
- **Modals & Overlays:** Pure institutional focus with a tinted scrim (`#0A1424` at 65% opacity), accompanied by a formal drop shadow (`rgba(15, 35, 71, 0.20) 0px 16px 32px`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a firm, structured shape language (`roundedness: 1`), keeping radiuses tight and measured.

### Geometric Rationale

- **Primary Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used on buttons, input elements, badges, and operational action triggers. This slight rounding softens hard digital edges while maintaining an architectural, official demeanor.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to membership credentials, charter records, and data cards.
- **Shield & Seal Masks:** Insignias, chapter crests, and eagle emblems use formal circular or heraldic shield outlines, framed by a continuous `1px` or `2px` metallic gold border.

## Components

### Buttons & Triggers

- **Primary (Formal Action):** Background `#0F2347`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #0F2347`. Padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. On hover, surface becomes `#1E3A8A`.
- **Secondary (Noble Outline):** Transparent background, text `#0F2347`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Text Link:** Weight 600, color `#0F2347`, text-decoration underline with 2px offset.

### Input Fields & Controls

- **Text Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8DEE4`, corner radius `4px`. Focused state replaces border with `#0F2347` and adds a subtle `2px` outer ring in `#D4AF37`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp `4px` radius for checkboxes; pure circle for radios. Selected state fills with `#0F2347` holding an inner gold `#D4AF37` glyph.

### Cards & Profiles

- **Member Credential Card:** Enclosed in white with a gold top rule (`3px solid #D4AF37`). Contains chapter insignia, member rank, eagle tenure, and fraternal ID number set in mono-spaced clarity.
- **Service Project / Charity Metric Card:** Structured with a bold numerical metric top-aligned in `Merriweather`, followed by a concise uppercase category label and a subtle horizontal separator.

### Badges & Institutional Chips

- **Fraternal Honor Chips:** Rounded `4px` pill, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`. Gold distinction uses `#FFFBEB` background with `#B45309` text and a `1px` border of `#D4AF37`. Chapter leadership chips utilize deep navy `#0F2347` with crisp white text.
